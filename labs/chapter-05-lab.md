# Chapter 5 Lab: Storage & Data Solutions Provisioning

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-understand walkthrough: design TerramEarth's telemetry pipeline from 2 million vehicles through Pub/Sub, Dataflow, and Bigtable, choosing correct storage and processing services

## Key takeaway

Data service selection is driven by consistency requirements, query pattern, and scale, not by familiarity with a particular database.
