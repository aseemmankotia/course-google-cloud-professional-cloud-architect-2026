# Chapter 11 Lab: SRE Practices, Observability & Disaster Recovery

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-understand walkthrough: define SLOs and an error budget policy for HRL's live-streaming platform, then design a multi-region DR strategy meeting a stated RTO/RPO

## Key takeaway

Reliability architecture decisions must be sized exactly to the stated RTO/RPO and SLO, never over-engineered beyond what the business actually requires.
